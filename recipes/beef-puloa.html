Beef puloa

Description

This is a meat dish, where the meat is slow cooked and the rice is cooked in the meat broth, mainly eaten in pakistan and afghanistan, originating from the turks

Ingredients

  • Beef
  • Onions
  • rice
  • spices

Steps

  1. Slow cook the beef
  2. Add the spices
  3. evaporate the water
  4. Cook the rice in the broth
